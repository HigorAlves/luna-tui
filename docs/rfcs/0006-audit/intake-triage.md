## Top gaps by impact

1. **No duplicate / related check at all** (I22-I29, I44, I47, I54). The whole purple node, its config, the merge and its event are absent. The RFC designed `intake_dedup` (`RFC:70`, `RFC:182`) but no wave carries it and no note drops it. Two reports of one problem become two cards and two pipelines.
2. **No "Context complete?" gate; `setup` / `awaiting_setup` mean something else** (I30, I36-I40). The states are the `luna.yaml` proposal gate (RFC-recorded). The diagram's job for them (resolve repo, project, owners, reporter, history, then ask for what is missing) has no home: a card with no repo fails inside triage (`pack-runner.ts:148-149`).
3. **The entry contract is thin** (I4-I7, I10, I11, I16, I17). No attachments, reporter, hints, origin machine, project or owners, and `source` is single and immutable. Everything downstream that needs them (outbound questions to a reporter, hint weighting, cross-team fast-lane test, merge) is blocked on this.
4. **Urgency model missing** (T8, T12-T14, T43). No sub-scores, weights, knock-outs or P0-P3; urgency is one rubric level. `kind` has no `incident`.
5. **`type_of_work` is `area`, hard-coded to this repo's folders** (T5, T40, T59). Wrong labels for any other repository, and the fast-lane exclusion cannot be computed.
6. **Fast lane is a prompt sentence, not a rule** (T58-T61). Not config, not machine-checked, no `type_of_work` or owners test; even the RFC's "from `triage_signals`, both at or above `proceed`" is unbuilt.
7. **No "Record complete?" decision, and the record is optional** (T50-T53, T38, T49). A triage answer with no valid draft passes to Plan with `task.record` null. No summary, version, `by` or `hints_used` on the record.
8. **Triage agent's bounds differ** (T27, T28, T34). No cap on question rounds, signals never re-run after answers, and the agent is a 30-turn Opus run rather than one call under 50k tokens.
9. **Outbound to the origin is partial** (T31, T56, T64, I40, I50). Question text is never posted and thread answers never read; a Slack-sourced card gets neither its conversation reply nor its archive notice in the origin thread.
10. **No triage or intake ground truth** (T65-T68, I44, I45). The record cannot be edited, an overridden outcome writes no truth, and `triage_signals` is the one decision pack with records but no truth source; its default is still "unmeasured". Confidence bars differ from the diagram's per-field values (T16) and the medium band is unused for triage (T18).

Also worth knowing: SLA defaults (I41, I42, T35, T36) are 1h reminder and no auto-archive, with one reason `sla_expired`; setting `sla.gate: 2d` and `sla.archive: 5d` in `luna.yaml` gives the diagram's timings but not its two reasons. Named events (I48, T69-T72) are generic `transition` / `stage.finished` events; a consumer can derive all but `card.merged` and `card.fast_lane`.

## Things built that contradict the diagram

- **Intake declines questions on its own** (I55): a GitHub or Jira issue classified `question` at or above the bar gets a canned comment and no card, while the diagram sends every source through a card and a human-tapped reply. `machine/src/application/intake.ts:88-96`, `machine/src/application/jira-intake.ts:154-159`.
- **`area` labels are luna-tui's own packages** inside a pack that runs for any repo (T5). `contracts/src/packs.ts:400-410`.
- **Signals run before the card exists** for GitHub and Jira (T3), and are never refreshed (T34), so the diagram's "② starts with signals, re-run on answer" holds only for TUI and Slack cards, once.
- **Triage can exit without a record** (T50). `machine/src/application/stages/triage-signals.ts:26-27,62-70`.
- **A conversation reply for a Slack card goes to the machine's bot-channel thread, not the thread it came from** (T56). `machine/src/application/threads.ts:131-151`.
- **The triage agent decides the fast lane and settles low-confidence signals itself** (T17, T58); the diagram has a config rule and a human ask. T17 is RFC-recorded; T58 is not, and falls short of the RFC's own rule.
- **The stage-side signals call ignores the repo's policy overrides** (T3). `machine/src/main.ts:416` against `machine/src/application/stages/triage-signals.ts:13`.
