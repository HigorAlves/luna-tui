## Top gaps by impact

1. **Stacked PRs are not handled anywhere** (S9, S35, S63): no `card.base`, no parent events, no retarget, rebase always onto the default branch, readiness never checks the PR's base. A PR whose base is another branch would be merged into that branch.
2. **The ship approval comes before the checklist** (S43): a high-risk approver attests at the preview gate, before review and final CI, and never sees readiness; the diagram puts the approval after "Ready?".
3. **No SLA on the monitored wait** (S4, S40): no 2-day reminder, no 5-day escalation to a release owner; a quiet PR sits silent for 14 days, then escalates.
4. **`re_plan` loses the reviewer's context** (S29): the thread is kept only as the task's reason; no stage input or prompt carries it to triage or the planner.
5. **`refresh_media` does not refresh the proof** (S28): Evidence re-runs on the old browser run and says so on the pack; it also costs another click at the ship gate.
6. **`base_moved` without a conflict is unhandled** (S19): a branch merely behind its base is neither rebased nor reported as a blocker.
7. **Merged SHA means two things** (S46): the machine's own direct merge records the PR head, not the merge commit, in `task.shipped`.
8. **Freeze and blocked never reach a person** (S20): one `stage.note`, no item.
9. **The retro's structured outputs go nowhere** (S56-S58): `recalibration[]`, `graphMisses[]` and `promptUpdates[]` are counts on a gate; `worth_learning` lacks override count, review rounds, cost and declared files (S53); no runbook or ADR proposals.
10. **A PR closed without a merge is not archived** (S60) and a rejected ship approval re-runs Evidence (S44): `pr_closed` is only a label a person can pick.
11. **Ground truth is thin outside Plan** (G1, G2, G4-G6): no dedup overrides, no triage edits or truth for `triage_signals`, no human-fix diff after an escalation, no dismissed or added findings, no evidence thumbs; the plan's ground-truth file is read by no eval.
12. **Event names are not the diagram's** (S61, E1-E6): one generic `transition` plus `stage.*`; only `task.shipped` and `pr.opened` are named. No deploy consumer and no DORA metric (S62, M1).
13. **No commit template, no merge configuration** (S38), and `fix_scope` returns no affected scenarios (S15).
